# Third-party source materials

This project retained source materials for private noncommercial research. Download availability is not a blanket permission to publish or sell the underlying data.

- GVR/REBGV owns its reports. Separately published economics CSV terms allow personal, educational and noncommercial research with attribution; those terms are not assumed to apply to every PDF.
- CREA permits private noncommercial analysis with attribution. The project did not treat the retained current HPI ZIP or derived reference observations as openly redistributable.
- Bank of Canada material was handled with attribution and source metadata.
- Statistics Canada metadata remains subject to its licence and product-specific exceptions.

No third-party publisher endorses this project. These notes document the research package's handling assumptions; they are not a grant of rights.
